darkroom

a quiet place where your taste develops. the machine works overnight; you walk in, judge contact sheets, and prints emerge. everything is warm near-black. the only color in the room is the safelight — and your images.

01

foreword

trove is a personal taste engine. you swipe through a pool of images, keep the ones that move you, let the rest go. the engine learns what you keep, names the facets of your taste, and dreams new images from it overnight. it runs for exactly one person, on one machine. local-first. no accounts, no cloud, no one else's algorithm.

this book exists because v1 didn't have one. without a locked direction, the interface thrashed — fifteen hundred lines of theme churn, three aesthetic restarts, a ui that kept asking what it wanted to be. v2 was rebuilt on real engineering research from pinterest and meta's retrieval work; the product logic is now sound. this book does the same for the brand: it goes deep once, decides once, and locks. everything the ui will ever need to know about how it looks, moves, and speaks lives here and in the design contract this book expands.

the contract is binding. the research behind it is below, so the decisions can be trusted without being reopened.

02

research — where we looked

design systems & engineering blogs

linear rebuilt its entire theme system on three lch variables — base color, contrast, saturation — and derives every surface from them; its later density work showed that different surfaces deserve different breathing room within one system. vercel's geist locks a small set of named type styles and a ten-step color scale with semantic aliases, so no one ever free-sizes text again. raycast runs a tight dark surface ladder with a single accent and absolute discipline about where it appears. apple's hig codifies dark-mode elevation-by-lightness and warns against high-frequency motion; material 3 publishes motion as tokens — named springs and durations, not ad-hoc values. and stripe gave the whole quiet-craft thesis a number: a redesign of one email, changing nothing but craft, converted twenty percent better. care is measurable.

linear.app/blog/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui · vercel.com/geist · raycast.com · developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines · m3.material.io/styles/motion · stripe.com/blog

studios

darkroom.engineering publishes its work as a timestamped activity log — process as interface. metalab splices two type voices in one headline and lets that be the whole identity. makemepulse ends sentences with an underscore_ and owns the tic. daybreak.studio's farewell page is the most instructive: almost nothing on screen, and the absence is the design. godly.studio navigates with inline text runs and a before/after toggle instead of chrome. locomotive allows itself exactly one quirk per project and executes it perfectly. 14islands paces long pages with caption-pair rhythm — image, two short mono lines, repeat. basement.studio assumes you have a keyboard and rewards you for it.

darkroom.engineering · metalab.com · makemepulse.com · daybreak.studio · godly.website · locomotive.ca · 14islands.com · basement.studio

galleries — what quiet looks like in 2025–26

reading across minimal.gallery, siteinspire, and the dark end of awwwards, the same patterns recur in the best restrained work: type scales collapsed to two sizes, technical mono annotations as the only accent, fades kept short and rare, and — increasingly — film grain as a deliberate texture. grain has become the anti-slop signal: the visible mark of a made thing in a feed of generated ones.

minimal.gallery · siteinspire.com · awwwards.com/websites/dark

products — the direct ancestors

cosmos.so renders saved images as a gapless, sharp-cornered masonry — one continuous surface, no cards. mymind's manifesto is written almost entirely in negations (no likes, no follows, no social pressure) and treats serendipity as a feature. savee's brand position is the refusal of the algorithm. are.na chose "connect" as its verb and built a culture from one word. pinterest's foundational principle is to let the content be the interface — the chrome recedes to near-invisibility. tinder's drag gives pre-commit feedback before you release, which is why a swipe feels like a judgment and not a button. photo mechanic, the tool working photographers actually cull with, auto-advances after every verdict — the next frame is already there. and spotify wrapped proved that data about yourself lands hardest as one insight per screen, never a dashboard.

cosmos.so · mymind.com · savee.it · are.na · pinterest.com · tinder.com · camerabits.com (photo mechanic) · spotify.com/wrapped

a note on honesty

deck.studio was requested as a reference and does not exist — dead dns, a domain parked since 2021. godly.studio and daybreak.studio covered the ground it was presumed to occupy. recording this here so the citation trail stays clean.

03

three explorations

three full art directions were developed as equals before one was chosen. each could carry the product. only one is true to it.

night archive

a private museum after closing. you walk the halls of your own collection with a flashlight; every image hangs with a small printed card beneath it. the interface is an institution of one — catalog index cards, contact-sheet caption pairs, the hush of a vault at night. dignified, slightly formal, deeply quiet.

type — manrope + mono caption pairs under every image
color — #1a1a1a neutral black, archival off-white text, no accent
layout — catalog index cards, generous plinth spacing
motion — 400ms fades only, nothing moves laterally

darkroom

the photographic process itself. warm near-black like a room lit only by a safelight; images develop in from grain and blur the way prints emerge in trays. the amber accent is reserved exclusively for the things you keep and love — the safelight glow on a print worth saving. the status page reads as lab notes. patient, warm, chemical.

type — manrope 300/400, system mono as lab-note metadata
color — warm near-black ladder (hue 75), one safelight accent, images as the only color
layout — one card in a void; gapless contact-sheet masonry
motion — develop-in (blur + brightness → clear), velocity-carrying exits, breathing opacity instead of spinners

the quiet ledger

text-first, are.na crossed with daybreak. typography carries everything; images do not appear until words summon them. the app reads as a notebook that happens to contain pictures — entries, indexes, marginalia. no accent color at all. the most radical of the three, and the most demanding of its reader.

type — typography is the entire interface, display sizes do the work
color — ground and ink only, zero accent
layout — single text column, images inline on demand
motion — near none; instant text, 120ms fades at most

the verdict — darkroom

the owner's measured taste decided it. his top facets, clustered by clip from his real keeps: motion blur · bokeh, harsh shadows · minimalist, long exposure · seascape, 35mm film, minimal architecture. that is a photographer's taste — light, time, and chemistry. an app about photographic taste should feel like the room where photographs are born. the metaphor isn't decoration here; it's the data.

darkroom borrows one thing from each rival: night archive's mono annotations become the lab-note metadata voice, and the quiet ledger's typographic confidence sets the scale of the headings.

lowercase, always

lowercase is the anti-corporate register — no title-case marketing voice, no shouting headers. it is also the consistent one: a system that never capitalizes can never capitalize wrongly. the rule has no exceptions, including error states. errors whisper too.

mono is for metadata only

the system mono stack (ui-monospace, "SF Mono", "Cascadia Mono", monospace) carries timestamps, counts, dimensions, and facet scores — the lab notes pencilled on the edge of a print. it never carries sentences. the moment mono starts speaking in prose, the lab-note voice is gone.

07

space & shape

scale
4px base. every gap, inset, and offset is a multiple of four.
masonry
2px gaps, radius 0 — one continuous surface of images, the cosmos move. the grid is a contact sheet, not a wall of cards.
cards & sheets
radius 8 maximum anywhere else.
deck card
radius 12 — the one held object in the app earns the softest corner.
grain
3% svg noise overlay on body, pointer-events none. the anti-slop signal: the visible mark of a made thing.

density follows mood

linear's lesson: one system, several densities. trove's contemplative surfaces — the deck, the taste page — are spacious, near-empty, slow to read. its productive surfaces — library, status — run denser, because culling and reading logs are work. same tokens, different breathing room. the room decides how close you stand to the wall.

08

motion

felt, not seen. motion in trove confirms physics or reveals a print; it never decorates.

// lib/motion.ts
export const snap   = { type: "spring", visualDuration: 0.25, bounce: 0 };
export const settle = { type: "spring", visualDuration: 0.3,  bounce: 0.15 };
export const morph  = { type: "spring", visualDuration: 0.45, bounce: 0.1 };
export const quiet  = [0.25, 1, 0.5, 1];  // micro fades 120–200ms
  • keyboard = 0ms. a keypress is a decision already made; animating it is latency.
  • ease-out bias. things arrive decisively and leave quietly.
  • everything interruptible. no animation ever blocks the next intent.
  • transform / opacity / filter only. nothing that touches layout.
  • reduced motion = crossfades. MotionConfig reducedMotion="user", globally.

choreography by surface

the deck drags 1:1 with a pendulum rotate; exits carry the release velocity, so a flick lands where the hand sent it. the under-card rises during the drag — the next frame is already developing. the library's lightbox is a shared-element morph, never a popup. dreams develop in: blur and dimmed brightness clearing over 600ms, the print surfacing in the tray. taste reveals are the one deliberately slow place in the app — 400 to 600ms, because a revelation should take a breath. and skip gets no feedback at all. absence is the feedback.

09

voice

lowercase, verb-first, brief. negations as manifesto — the mymind move — because saying what you refuse is the fastest way to say what you are. verdicts are named keep / let go in the ui (the schema keeps keep/skip; people aren't schemas). empty states prescribe one action and never apologize twice.

10

the surfaces

/ — the deck

ground is bg0, the void. one card floating at max-width 420, faint keyboard hints in the corners. pre-commit feedback on right-drag: a 1px safelight inner ring fading in with distance. nothing appears on left-drag. commit only on release; exits carry velocity. sessions are decks of 30 with thin progress dots — mono "14 left", never a big total. undo is free: the u key plus a last-5 thumbnail tray at the bottom edge. a kept count ticks via numberflow with a ✦ glyph that fades in and out.

patterns — daybreak absence · tinder pre-commit drag · photo mechanic auto-advance · free undo
/library — the contact sheet

gapless sharp masonry, 2px and radius 0. hover dims the neighbors to 0.75 and fades in a mono metadata strip — the image itself never moves. click opens a shared-element lightbox: image left, mono metadata rail right, a "more like this" strip of six clip neighbors so there is never a dead end. density toggles on - and + keys. the search placeholder is seeded from a real facet keyword.

patterns — cosmos gapless surface · savee density · pinterest never-a-dead-end lightbox
/taste — the revelation

not a database — a profile of you. full-bleed facet rows with the medoid as hero, developing in on view. display-size lowercase labels, one identity line computed from real data. importance bars scale in once, over 600ms. facets are click-to-rename. mono annotations everywhere: members, importance, last fed.

patterns — wrapped one-insight-per-screen · godly before/after spirit
/dreams

same masonry as the library. dream cards develop in — blur(12) and brightness(0.4) clearing over 600ms, staggered. ✦ marks the dreams. a "dream from your taste" chip stays. the footer states the boundary: kept dreams never touch retrieval taste.

patterns — darkroom develop-in · honest provenance marking
/status — lab notes

a reverse-chron one-column activity log with mono timestamps: "01:12 kept unsplash-x → fed harsh shadows" · "01:10 retrained taste · 11 facets". stats above as numberflow numerals. errors sit inline in the log, safelight-tinted — the only other thing allowed to glow, because failures matter as much as keeps. a quiet dot-row notes "judged 5 of the last 7 days" with no streak guilt.

patterns — darkroom.engineering activity log
every page

the footer is an index — a lowercase text-run, swipe / library / taste / dreams / status — followed by one negation line: "local-first. no cloud. no one else's algorithm." nav stays top and hints at keys.

patterns — godly inline text nav · mymind negation manifesto

stack

chosen from the vetted designengineer.tools list, which favors agentic tools — ones an agent can drive directly. next.js 16 and motion stay from v2. four additions, three refusals, nine dependencies total.

keep
next.js 16 · motion — both agentic picks, both already carrying the app
add
@number-flow/react for the stats numerals · oklch tokens as plain css custom properties · system mono (zero bytes shipped) · motion layoutid morphs for the lightbox
refuse
shadcn — the custom css here is smaller than the abstraction · lottie — v1 burned on it · lenis — windowed masonry and native scroll are already smooth

nine dependencies is the budget. every addition after this must displace something.
